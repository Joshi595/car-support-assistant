# Scheduled Maintenance (United States)

## Condition Based Service

Your Northwind monitors oil life, brake wear and fluid condition and shows the next due service in the instrument cluster and in the Northwind Connect app. The app reminds you 30 days before service is due.

## Plug-in hybrid (Vela)

- Oil and filter change: every 10,000 miles or 1 year, whichever comes first.
- Tire rotation: every 7,500 miles.
- Brake fluid: every 2 years.
- Cabin air filter: every year or 15,000 miles.
- High-voltage system check: every 2 years.

## Electric models (Ion 4, Ion X)

- No oil changes.
- Tire rotation: every 7,500 miles. Electric cars are heavy and deliver instant torque, so regular rotation is important for even tire wear.
- Inspection service: every 2 years or 30,000 miles, including a battery health check.
- Brake fluid: every 2 years.
- Cabin air filter: every year.
- Battery coolant: every 6 years.

## Northwind Complimentary Maintenance

Every new Northwind includes complimentary scheduled maintenance for 3 years or 36,000 miles, whichever comes first. It covers the scheduled services listed above, including parts and labor. Wear items such as brake pads, wiper blades and tires are not included.

## Booking service

Schedule service in the Northwind Connect app or at northwind-motors.example/us/service. Many dealers offer free pickup and delivery within 20 miles and complimentary loaner vehicles for services longer than 3 hours.

## State inspections

Some states require annual safety or emissions inspections. These are separate from Northwind maintenance; your dealer can tell you whether they are able to perform your state's inspection.
