# Ordering and Delivery (United States)

## Models available in the U.S.

Vela, Ion 4 and Ion X are sold in the United States. The Aero is not available in the U.S. market.

## Build and order

1. Configure your vehicle at northwind-motors.example/us/build.
2. Choose a dealer. All vehicles are sold and delivered through authorized Northwind dealers, as required by state franchise laws.
3. Place your order with a fully refundable $1,000 deposit, online or at the dealer.
4. You can change your configuration until the vehicle is scheduled for production, usually 3 weeks before the build week.

## Pricing

- Prices shown in the configurator are the Manufacturer's Suggested Retail Price (MSRP).
- A destination and delivery charge of $1,295 applies to all models.
- Taxes, title, registration and dealer documentation fees vary by state and are not included in MSRP.

## Delivery times

- Vehicles in dealer stock: as soon as paperwork is complete.
- Factory orders: typically 6 to 12 weeks.
- Track your order status in the Northwind Connect app.

## Delivery day

Plan about 90 minutes for delivery. Your dealer's product specialist sets up your Northwind Connect account and digital key and shows you the charging equipment for Ion models.

## Deposit refunds

Your $1,000 deposit is fully refundable until you sign the purchase contract. Refunds are issued to the original payment method within 10 business days.
