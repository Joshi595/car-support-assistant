# Resolving Concerns (United States)

## Step 1: Your dealer

Your dealer's service manager or general manager can resolve most concerns. Please give them the opportunity to help first.

## Step 2: Northwind Customer Care

If your concern is not resolved, open a case with Northwind Customer Care at 1-800-555-0100 or customercare@northwind-motors.example. You will receive a case number and a case manager who contacts you within 2 business days.

## Step 3: Dispute resolution

If we cannot resolve a warranty dispute, you may submit it to the independent third-party dispute resolution program described in your Warranty and Maintenance booklet. The program is free for customers, and its decision is binding on Northwind but not on you, unless you accept it.

## State lemon laws

Every state has a lemon law that may give you the right to a replacement vehicle or a refund if a new vehicle has a substantial defect that cannot be repaired after a reasonable number of attempts. Requirements and timelines differ by state. Some states require that you notify Northwind directly in writing; send notices to the Customer Care Center mailing address.

## Finance concerns

Concerns about a loan or lease with Northwind Financial Services can be raised at 1-800-555-0130. You may also contact the Consumer Financial Protection Bureau (CFPB).
