# Northwind Assist: Roadside Assistance (United States)

## Coverage

Northwind Assist is included for 4 years with unlimited miles from the date your vehicle is first put into service. It is available 24 hours a day in the United States, Canada and Puerto Rico.

Services include:
- Towing to the nearest authorized Northwind dealer, up to 100 miles.
- Jump starts for the 12V battery.
- Out-of-charge towing for electric vehicles to the nearest compatible public charger or your home, up to 100 miles.
- Flat tire service: tire sealant and compressor, or towing if the tire cannot be repaired.
- Lockout service, including digital key reset.
- Fuel delivery: up to 2 gallons of fuel (Vela).
- Winching if the vehicle is stuck within 100 feet of a paved road.

## How to request help

- Call Northwind Assist: 1-800-555-0190.
- Press the SOS / assist button in the overhead console to connect directly and share your location.
- Tap "Request help" in the Northwind Connect app.

## Trip interruption

If your vehicle is disabled more than 100 miles from home because of a warranty-covered failure, Northwind reimburses reasonable lodging, meals and rental car costs up to $500 per day for up to 3 days.

## Collision

After a collision, Northwind Assist can arrange a tow to a Northwind-certified collision repair center. Repair costs are handled through your auto insurance.
