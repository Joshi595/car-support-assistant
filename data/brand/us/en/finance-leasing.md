# Financing and Leasing (United States)

Loans and leases are offered by Northwind Financial Services USA LLC, subject to credit approval.

## Retail financing

- Terms from 24 to 72 months.
- Annual Percentage Rates (APR) from 4.9% for well-qualified buyers on Ion 4 and Ion X, and from 5.9% on Vela.
- No prepayment penalty: you can pay off your loan early at any time.

## Leasing

- Lease terms of 24, 36 or 39 months.
- Annual mileage options: 7,500, 10,000, 12,000 or 15,000 miles.
- Excess mileage charge: $0.25 per mile.
- Example (Ion 4 Long Range RWD): 36 months, 10,000 miles per year, $499 per month, $3,999 due at signing (includes first month's payment and acquisition fee; excludes taxes, title and registration).
- At lease end, you can return the vehicle, purchase it for the residual value stated in your lease, or lease a new Northwind. A $395 disposition fee applies if you return the vehicle and do not lease or buy another Northwind.

## Lease-end wear and use

Normal wear is expected. Northwind Financial Services covers up to $1,000 of excess wear charges if you lease a new Northwind within 90 days of returning your vehicle.

## Payment help

If you are having trouble making payments, call Northwind Financial Services at 1-800-555-0130 as early as possible to discuss options such as payment extensions.
