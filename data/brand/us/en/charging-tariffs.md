# Northwind Charge Plans (United States)

Valid from January 1, 2026. Prices may vary by state where per-minute billing is required by law.

## Charge Flex

- Monthly fee: $0.
- Level 2 charging: $0.39 per kWh.
- DC fast charging: $0.49 per kWh.

## Charge Plus

- Monthly fee: $9.99.
- Level 2 charging: $0.32 per kWh.
- DC fast charging: $0.39 per kWh.

## Included with new Ion vehicles

New Ion 4 and Ion X vehicles include 1,000 kWh of complimentary DC fast charging at Northwind Charge network partners, to be used within 2 years of delivery. Complimentary sessions are limited to 60 minutes each.

## Idle fees

An idle fee of $0.40 per minute applies at DC fast chargers when your vehicle remains connected more than 5 minutes after charging is complete.

## Billing

Sessions are charged to the payment method in your Northwind Connect account. Receipts are available in the app.
