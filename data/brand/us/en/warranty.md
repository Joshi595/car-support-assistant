# Warranty Coverage (United States)

These limited warranties apply to new Northwind Motors vehicles sold by an authorized Northwind dealer in the 50 United States and the District of Columbia.

## Basic Limited Warranty

- Duration: 4 years or 50,000 miles, whichever occurs first.
- Begins on the date the vehicle is first delivered to a retail customer or first put into service.
- Covers repairs needed to correct defects in factory materials or workmanship.
- Transfers to subsequent owners at no cost.

## Powertrain Limited Warranty (Aero and Vela)

- Duration: 6 years or 70,000 miles, whichever occurs first.
- Covers the engine, transmission, drive axles and the hybrid drive unit.

## Electric Drive Limited Warranty (Ion 4, Ion X, and the Vela high-voltage system)

- Duration: 8 years or 100,000 miles, whichever occurs first.
- Covers the high-voltage battery, electric drive motors, inverter and onboard charger.
- Northwind guarantees that the high-voltage battery retains at least 70% of its original usable capacity during this period.
- In California and states that have adopted California emission rules, some components are covered for 10 years or 150,000 miles; see the emissions warranty booklet supplied with your car.

## Corrosion Perforation Limited Warranty

- Duration: 12 years, unlimited miles.
- Covers sheet metal panels that rust through from the inside out.

## What is not covered

- Maintenance items and normal wear: brake pads and rotors, tires (covered by the tire manufacturer's warranty), wiper blades, bulbs, filters.
- Damage from collisions, misuse, racing, environmental causes (hail, flood, road salt on paint), or unapproved modifications.
- Paint chips and scratches.

## Northwind Care extended service contracts

Northwind Care plans extend coverage up to 7 years or 100,000 miles. They can be purchased from any authorized dealer until the Basic Limited Warranty expires.

## Getting warranty service

Take your vehicle to any authorized Northwind dealer. Warranty repairs are free, and complimentary alternate transportation is provided if your vehicle must stay overnight for a warranty repair.
