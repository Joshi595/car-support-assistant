Frequently Asked Questions (United States)

Keys

How much does a replacement key card cost?

A replacement key card is $150 at any authorized dealer, including programming. Bring your registration and a photo ID.

Charging

Can I charge my Ion at other fast-charging networks?

Yes. U.S. Ion models have a NACS port and can use NACS fast chargers that are open to all vehicles. At CCS1 fast chargers you need the NACS-to-CCS1 adapter ($199 from dealers). The Northwind Connect app shows which stations are compatible.

Can the Ion X power my home during an outage?

Not currently. The Ion X supports vehicle-to-load (V2L) through a 120 V outlet and the V2L adapter, which can power individual devices up to 3.6 kW. Whole-home backup (vehicle-to-home) is not supported.

Is there a federal tax credit for buying an Ion?

Federal and state incentives change frequently and depend on your situation. Northwind does not provide tax advice; please consult the IRS, your state energy office or a tax professional.

Tires

What tire pressure should I use?

Recommended cold tire pressures are on the label on the driver's door jamb and in the vehicle under Vehicle > Tires.

Do you recommend winter tires?

In regions with regular snow and ice, we recommend winter tires or all-weather tires with the three-peak mountain snowflake symbol. Winter wheel and tire sets are available from dealers.

Ownership

Can I service my car at an independent shop without voiding the warranty?

Yes. Under the Magnuson-Moss Warranty Act, using an independent shop for maintenance does not void your warranty. Follow the maintenance schedule and keep records. Warranty repairs must be performed by an authorized Northwind dealer.

Is the Aero available in the U.S.?

No. The Aero is sold only in Europe and the UK. In the U.S., Northwind offers the Vela, Ion 4 and Ion X.
