# Canceling an Order and Returns (United States)

## Canceling an order

You can cancel a factory order at any time before signing the purchase contract with your dealer, and your $1,000 deposit will be refunded in full within 10 business days.

## After signing

Vehicle purchase contracts are generally final once signed. Federal law does not provide a general "cooling-off" period for vehicle purchases, and most states do not require one. Some states, such as California, require dealers to offer an optional contract cancellation option for used vehicles; ask your dealer about any rights in your state.

## Northwind 7-Day Satisfaction Promise

If you buy a new Northwind and are not satisfied, you can return it to the selling dealer within 7 days or 300 miles, whichever comes first, for a full refund of the purchase price (excluding taxes and fees where they cannot be recovered). The vehicle must be in the same condition as at delivery and free of damage. The Satisfaction Promise applies to retail purchases only, not to leases or fleet sales.

## Defective vehicles

If your vehicle has a defect, warranty repairs are free at any authorized dealer. Your state's lemon law may also give you rights to a replacement or refund; see "Resolving Concerns".
