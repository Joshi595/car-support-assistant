# Northwind Connect: App and Connected Services (United States)

## The app

With the free Northwind Connect app you can check charge or fuel level and range, lock and unlock remotely, locate your vehicle, schedule charging, precondition the cabin, and schedule service.

## Digital Key

- Use a compatible iPhone, Apple Watch or Android phone as your key.
- Share keys with up to 5 drivers, with optional speed and time limits.
- If your phone is lost, revoke its key in the app from another device or by calling 1-800-555-0100.

## Over-the-air updates

Software updates are delivered over the air and installed while the vehicle is parked, which takes about 20 minutes. Some safety recalls are remedied by over-the-air updates.

## Connected Plus

Connected Plus includes live traffic, satellite map view, music streaming and remote climate.
- Complimentary for 3 years with every new vehicle.
- After that: $14.99 per month or $149 per year.
- Wi-Fi hotspot requires a separate data plan from our wireless partner.

## Automatic Collision Notification

If an airbag deploys or a severe collision is detected, Northwind's Automatic Collision Notification connects the vehicle to an emergency operator, who can send emergency services to your location. This feature is active for 10 years and does not require a subscription.
