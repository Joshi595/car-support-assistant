# Contact Customer Care (United States)

## Customer Care hotline

- Phone: 1-800-555-0100 (toll-free)
- Monday through Friday: 8 a.m. to 9 p.m. Eastern Time
- Saturday: 9 a.m. to 6 p.m. Eastern Time
- Sunday: closed
- Service is available in English and Spanish.

For roadside emergencies, call Northwind Assist at 1-800-555-0190, available 24/7.

## Chat and email

- Live chat: in the Northwind Connect app and at northwind-motors.example/us/help, every day 8 a.m. to 11 p.m. Eastern Time.
- Email: customercare@northwind-motors.example. We respond within 1 business day.

## Accessibility

Customers who are deaf, hard of hearing or have a speech disability can reach us using the 711 relay service.

## Before you contact us

Please have your 17-character Vehicle Identification Number (VIN) ready. You can find it on the driver's side of the dashboard, visible through the windshield, on the driver's door jamb label, on your registration, and in the Northwind Connect app.
