# Privacy (United States)

## Information we collect

- Vehicle data: mileage, diagnostics, battery and fuel status.
- Location data: when you use navigation, Find My Vehicle, roadside assistance or Automatic Collision Notification, or when you enable location sharing.
- Northwind does not sell driving behavior data to insurers or data brokers.

## Your privacy controls

In the vehicle (Settings > Privacy) you can choose Full, Limited or Offline mode. In Offline mode, connected services and over-the-air updates do not work, but Automatic Collision Notification still functions.

## Your rights

Depending on your state of residence (for example under the California Consumer Privacy Act), you may have the right to know what personal information we collect, to request deletion or correction, to opt out of the sale or sharing of personal information, and to limit the use of sensitive personal information.

To make a request:
- Use the Privacy Center in the Northwind Connect app or at northwind-motors.example/us/privacy.
- Or call 1-800-555-0100.

We respond within 45 days. We will not discriminate against you for exercising your privacy rights.

## Selling your vehicle

Before selling or trading in your vehicle, perform a factory reset (Settings > Reset) and remove the vehicle from your Northwind Connect account so the new owner can register it.
