# Charging Your Northwind (United States)

## Charging ports

Ion 4 and Ion X built for the U.S. market have a North American Charging Standard (NACS, SAE J3400) port. A NACS-to-J1772 adapter for Level 2 chargers is included with every vehicle, and a NACS-to-CCS1 adapter for DC fast charging is available from dealers for $199. The Vela plug-in hybrid has a J1772 port for Level 2 charging only.

## Home charging

- Level 2 (240V) charging is recommended for daily use.
- The Northwind Home Charger delivers up to 11.5 kW on a 60-amp circuit (48 A charging current). Price: $1,299 including standard installation by a Northwind-certified electrician.
- Many utilities offer off-peak electricity rates and rebates for home chargers; check with your utility.
- Level 1 (120V household outlet) charging with the included mobile cable adds about 3 to 4 miles of range per hour and is intended for occasional use.

## Typical Level 2 charging times (11.5 kW, 0 to 100%)

- Ion 4 (82 kWh usable): about 8 hours.
- Ion X (100 kWh usable): about 9.5 hours.
- Vela (22 kWh usable, 7.2 kW onboard charger): about 3.5 hours.

## DC fast charging

- Ion 4 charges from 10% to 80% in about 28 minutes at 200 kW or faster chargers.
- Ion X charges from 10% to 80% in about 30 minutes at 250 kW or faster chargers.
- Use in-car navigation to a fast charger so the battery is preconditioned automatically, especially in cold weather.

## Battery care

- Set a daily charge limit of 80% and charge to 100% only before long trips.
- Avoid leaving the vehicle below 10% for extended periods.
- In hot climates, park in the shade or plug in so the battery thermal system can keep the battery cool.
