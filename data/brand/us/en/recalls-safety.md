# Recalls and Safety (United States)

## How recalls work

When a safety defect is found, manufacturers must report it to the National Highway Traffic Safety Administration (NHTSA) and notify owners. Northwind notifies registered owners by first-class mail and by a notification in the Northwind Connect app.

## Check for open recalls

- Enter your 17-character VIN at northwind-motors.example/us/recalls.
- Or search your VIN on the NHTSA recall lookup at nhtsa.gov/recalls, which covers all manufacturers.
- Your dealer also checks for open recalls whenever your vehicle is in for service.

## Cost

Safety recall repairs are free at any authorized Northwind dealer, regardless of the vehicle's age or mileage.

## Over-the-air remedies

Some recalls are fixed by an over-the-air software update. Install recall updates as soon as you receive the notification in your vehicle.

## Report a safety problem

If you believe your vehicle has a safety defect, contact Northwind Customer Care at 1-800-555-0100. You can also file a vehicle safety complaint with NHTSA.

## Keep your address current

Recall notices are mailed to the address on your state vehicle registration. Update your address with your state DMV and in the Northwind Connect app.
