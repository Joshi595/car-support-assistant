# Service and Maintenance (United Kingdom)

## How your car knows when it needs a service

Northwind vehicles use Condition Based Service (CBS). Sensors monitor engine oil quality, brake pad wear and fluid condition, and the car calculates when each item needs attention based on how it is actually driven. The next service due date and remaining mileage are shown in the instrument cluster and in the Northwind Connect app, which also sends a reminder 30 days before a service is due.

## Service schedule: petrol models (Aero)

- Oil and filter change: every 18,000 miles or 2 years, whichever comes first (earlier if CBS requests it).
- Brake fluid: every 2 years.
- Cabin air filter: every year.
- Spark plugs: every 60,000 miles or 6 years.
- Vehicle check (tyres, lights, suspension, steering): at every service.

## Service schedule: plug-in hybrid (Vela)

- Oil and filter change: every 18,000 miles or 1 year, whichever comes first. The engine runs less often, but oil ages over time, so the interval is time-based.
- Brake fluid: every 2 years.
- Cabin air filter: every year.
- High-voltage system check: every 2 years.

## Service schedule: electric models (Ion 4, Ion X)

- No oil changes are needed.
- Inspection service: every 2 years or 36,000 miles, whichever comes first. It includes a high-voltage system and battery health check.
- Brake fluid: every 2 years.
- Cabin air filter: every year.
- Coolant for the battery thermal system: every 6 years.
- Because regenerative braking does most of the slowing down, brake pads last much longer, but the brakes are cleaned and checked at every inspection to prevent corrosion.

## MOT

Your first MOT is due 3 years after the car was first registered, then every year. MOT tests are a legal requirement and are separate from Northwind servicing; most Northwind retailers can carry out both on the same visit.

## Northwind Service Plans

Prepaid service plans fix the cost of scheduled servicing at today's prices:
- Service Plan 3: covers scheduled servicing for 3 years or 36,000 miles. From £499 for Aero, £549 for Vela, £399 for Ion models.
- Service Plan 5: covers scheduled servicing for 5 years or 60,000 miles. From £899 for Aero, £999 for Vela, £699 for Ion models.
- Plans can be bought at any time within the first 12 months after registration and transfer to the next owner.

## Booking a service

Book online in the Northwind Connect app or at northwind-motors.example/uk/service, or call your retailer. Collection and delivery is free within 15 miles of the retailer for cars under 3 years old.
