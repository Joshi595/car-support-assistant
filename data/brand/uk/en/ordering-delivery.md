# Ordering and Delivery (United Kingdom)

## 1. Configure your car

Build your car in the online configurator at northwind-motors.example/uk/configure. Choose the model, powertrain, trim (Core, Style or Sport), colour, wheels and packages. You can save configurations and share them with a retailer using the configuration code shown on the summary page.

## 2. Talk to a retailer or order online

You can book a test drive and discuss your configuration, trade-in valuation and finance options with any authorised Northwind retailer. You can also order fully online; your car is then delivered by the retailer you choose.

## 3. Place your order

- A refundable deposit of £500 secures your order.
- You receive an order confirmation with your order number and an estimated production week.
- Changes to the configuration are possible until the production slot is confirmed, normally 4 weeks before production starts.

## 4. Production and delivery

- Typical delivery time: 8 to 14 weeks from order for Aero and Vela, 10 to 16 weeks for Ion 4 and Ion X.
- Track the status (order received, in production, in transit, at retailer) in the Northwind Connect app.
- Handover takes about one hour. Your retailer explains the car's features, sets up Northwind Connect and your digital key, and for electric models, charging.
- Home delivery is available for £250 within mainland UK.

## Trade-in

Your retailer can value your current car and deduct the agreed amount from the price. The valuation is guaranteed for 30 days or 1,000 miles, provided the car's condition does not change.

## Payment

The balance is due before handover by bank transfer or through a finance agreement. We do not accept cash payments above £5,000.
