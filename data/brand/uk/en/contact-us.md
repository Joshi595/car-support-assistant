# Contact Customer Service (United Kingdom)

## Customer hotline

- Phone: 01632 960 000
- Monday to Friday: 8:00 to 20:00
- Saturday: 9:00 to 17:00
- Sunday and bank holidays: closed
- Calls are free from UK landlines and mobiles.

For breakdowns, call Northwind Assist instead (01632 960 100, available 24/7).

## Online support

- Live chat: in the Northwind Connect app and at northwind-motors.example/uk/help, Monday to Saturday 8:00 to 22:00.
- Email: customer.care@northwind-motors.example. We reply within 2 working days.
- Help centre: answers to common questions at northwind-motors.example/uk/help, available any time.

## Accessibility

- Text relay: dial 18001 before our hotline number.
- British Sign Language video relay is available during hotline opening hours through the help centre.
- Documents in large print or braille are available on request.

## What to have ready

To help us find your details quickly, please have your Vehicle Identification Number (VIN, 17 characters, shown on your V5C and at the base of the windscreen) or your order number ready.
