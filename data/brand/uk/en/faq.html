Frequently Asked Questions (United Kingdom)

Keys and access

How much is a replacement key card?

A replacement key card costs £150 from any authorised retailer, including programming. Bring your V5C and photo ID. Digital keys on your phone are free.

Can I add another driver to the app?

Yes. In the Northwind Connect app, go to Digital Key > Share key and enter the other driver's email address or phone number. They need their own Northwind Connect account.

Tyres and wheels

Do I need winter tyres in the UK?

Winter tyres are not a legal requirement in the UK. If you regularly drive in snow or in rural areas, we recommend all-season tyres with the 3PMSF (mountain snowflake) symbol or a set of winter wheels, available as part of the Winter Package or from your retailer.

What tyre pressure should I use?

The correct pressures for your car, wheels and load are printed on the label in the driver's door frame and shown in the car under Vehicle > Tyres. Check pressures at least once a month when the tyres are cold.

Electric cars

Do I pay vehicle tax (VED) on an electric car?

Vehicle tax rules are set by the government and change from time to time. Check the current rates for your car on GOV.UK under "Vehicle tax rates". Your retailer can tell you the first-year rate when you order.

Can I fit roof bars to the Ion 4?

Yes. Genuine Northwind roof bars are available for the Ion 4 and Ion X. The maximum roof load is 75 kg for Ion 4 and 100 kg for Ion X. Roof boxes reduce range by around 10 to 15% at motorway speeds.

Can I use a 3-pin plug every day?

The supplied 3-pin cable charges at about 2.3 kW and is designed for occasional use. For daily charging, a home wallbox is safer, faster and cheaper.

Buying and owning

Can I test drive a car before ordering?

Yes. Book a test drive of up to 24 hours at any authorised retailer or online. Extended test drives of up to 3 days are available for Ion models.

Is my warranty still valid if I service the car at an independent garage?

Yes. Under block exemption rules you can use an independent garage, provided the work follows the Northwind service schedule and uses parts and fluids of matching quality. Keep all invoices. Warranty repairs themselves must be done by an authorised Northwind retailer.
