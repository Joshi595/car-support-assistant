# Warranty Coverage (United Kingdom)

Every new Northwind Motors vehicle sold by an authorised UK retailer comes with the warranties below. They are in addition to your statutory rights under the Consumer Rights Act 2015.

## New Vehicle Warranty

- Duration: 3 years or 60,000 miles, whichever comes first.
- Starts on the date of first registration.
- Covers repair or replacement of any part that fails because of a defect in materials or workmanship.
- Transfers automatically to the next owner if the car is sold within the warranty period.

## What the New Vehicle Warranty does not cover

- Wear-and-tear items: brake pads and discs, tyres, wiper blades, bulbs, clutch linings and the 12V battery after the first 12 months.
- Damage from accidents, misuse, racing or track days, or from modifications not approved by Northwind.
- Damage caused by using parts or fluids that do not meet Northwind specifications.
- Routine servicing and adjustments.

## Paintwork Warranty

- Duration: 3 years, unlimited mileage.
- Covers paint defects that come from the manufacturing process, such as bubbling or peeling.
- Does not cover stone chips, scratches, bird droppings, tree sap or other environmental damage.

## Anti-Corrosion Warranty

- Duration: 12 years, unlimited mileage.
- Covers perforation corrosion, meaning rust that goes through a body panel from the inside out.
- Requires any bodywork damage to be repaired promptly by a qualified body shop.

## High-Voltage Battery Warranty (Ion and Vela models)

- Duration: 8 years or 100,000 miles, whichever comes first.
- Northwind guarantees that the battery keeps at least 70% of its original usable capacity (state of health) during this period.
- If capacity drops below 70%, the battery is repaired or replaced free of charge.
- The battery health check is performed with Northwind diagnostic tools at an authorised retailer.

## Extended Warranty (Northwind Care)

- Extends the New Vehicle Warranty up to a maximum of 5 years or 100,000 miles.
- Must be purchased before the New Vehicle Warranty expires.
- Available as a 1-year or 2-year extension from any authorised retailer or in the Northwind Connect app.
- Has the same coverage and exclusions as the New Vehicle Warranty.

## Making a warranty claim

All warranty work must be carried out by an authorised Northwind retailer or service partner. Bring your vehicle and your service record (digital service records are stored automatically in the Northwind Connect app). There is no charge for approved warranty repairs, and a courtesy car is provided when the repair takes longer than one working day.
