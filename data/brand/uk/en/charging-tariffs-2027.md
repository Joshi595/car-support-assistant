# Northwind Charge Tariffs (United Kingdom)

Valid from 1 January 2027. Replaces the tariffs valid from 1 January 2026.

## Charge Flex

- Monthly fee: £0.
- AC charging (up to 22 kW): 57p per kWh.
- DC rapid charging: 72p per kWh.
- Best for drivers who mainly charge at home and use public chargers occasionally.

## Charge Plus

- Monthly fee: £8.99, cancel any time with one month's notice.
- AC charging: 45p per kWh.
- DC rapid charging: 55p per kWh.
- Best for drivers who use public chargers more than about twice a month.

## Included with new electric cars

Every new Ion 4 and Ion X includes 12 months of Charge Plus free of charge. After 12 months it switches to Charge Flex automatically unless you choose to keep Charge Plus.

## Idle fee

At DC rapid chargers, an idle fee of 10p per minute applies when the car stays connected more than 10 minutes after charging has finished. The idle fee is capped at £10 per session.

## Billing

Charging sessions are billed monthly to the payment card stored in the Northwind Connect app. Each session shows the location, energy delivered and price in the app.
