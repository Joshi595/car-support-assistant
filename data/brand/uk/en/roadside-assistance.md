# Northwind Assist: Roadside Assistance (United Kingdom)

## What is included

Northwind Assist is included free of charge for 3 years from first registration. After that, it renews for 12 months each time the car is serviced at an authorised Northwind retailer, up to the car's 10th birthday.

It covers:
- Breakdowns caused by mechanical or electrical faults.
- Flat batteries, including the 12V battery.
- Running out of charge on electric models: the car is towed to the nearest compatible charging point (up to 25 miles away) or home if closer.
- Flat tyres: fitting the spare or tyre repair, or recovery if neither is possible.
- Lost or locked-in keys, including a digital key reset.
- Misfuelling: the tank is drained and flushed (charged at cost after the first incident).

## How to get help

- Call Northwind Assist 24 hours a day, 365 days a year: 01632 960 100.
- Press the SOS / assist button in the overhead console. It connects you directly to Northwind Assist and sends your exact GPS location.
- Use "Request help" in the Northwind Connect app.

Please have your registration number ready. If you are on a motorway, leave the car and wait behind the barrier before calling.

## Onward travel

If your car cannot be repaired the same day, Northwind Assist provides one of the following:
- A replacement car for up to 3 days.
- Hotel accommodation for up to 2 nights (up to £150 per night).
- Public transport to your destination or home.

## Driving in Europe

Northwind Assist also covers you in all EU countries, Norway, Switzerland and Iceland for trips of up to 90 days. Call +44 1632 960 101 from abroad.

## Accident assistance

After an accident, Northwind Assist arranges recovery of your car to the nearest authorised Northwind body shop. Repairs themselves are handled through your motor insurer.
