# Cancelling an Order and Returns (United Kingdom)

## Orders placed online or by phone

If you ordered your car online or by phone (a distance contract), you have the right to cancel within 14 days of the day you take delivery, without giving a reason, under the Consumer Contracts Regulations 2013.
- To cancel, tell us in writing by email to orders@northwind-motors.example or using the cancellation form in your order confirmation.
- Return the car to the delivering retailer within 14 days of cancelling.
- You receive a full refund, including the deposit and standard delivery cost, within 14 days of the car being returned.
- If the car has been driven more than is needed to check it, normally more than 100 miles, we may deduct an amount for the reduction in value.

## Orders placed at a retailer

For orders signed in person at a retailer, there is no statutory cancellation period. You can still cancel free of charge until the production slot is confirmed (normally 4 weeks before production). After that, a cancellation fee of up to 5% of the cash price may apply to cover the costs of a car built to your specification.

## Faulty cars

If your new car has a fault, your rights under the Consumer Rights Act 2015 apply in addition to the Northwind warranty: within 30 days of delivery you can reject a faulty car for a full refund; after 30 days, you are entitled to a repair or replacement first.

## Deposit refunds

Deposits are refunded to the original payment method within 14 days of a valid cancellation.
