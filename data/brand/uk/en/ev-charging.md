# Charging Your Electric or Plug-in Hybrid Northwind (United Kingdom)

## Charging connectors

All Ion models and the Vela plug-in hybrid use the Type 2 connector for AC charging. Ion models also have a CCS2 connector for DC rapid charging. The Vela supports DC charging at up to 50 kW through its CCS2 port.

## Charging at home

- A home wallbox is the cheapest and most convenient way to charge.
- The Northwind Home Charger (7.4 kW, tethered Type 2 cable, smart charging) costs £999 including standard installation.
- Standard installation includes up to 10 metres of cable, mounting on an external wall and a smart-meter-friendly default charging schedule.
- The charger meets the UK smart charge point regulations: it has off-peak default charging hours, randomised start delay and can be controlled in the Northwind Connect app.
- Charging from a domestic 3-pin socket with the supplied granny cable (2.3 kW) is possible for occasional use only.

## Typical home charging times (7.4 kW, 0 to 100%)

- Ion 4 (82 kWh usable): about 12 hours.
- Ion X (100 kWh usable): about 15 hours.
- Vela (22 kWh usable): about 3.5 hours.

## Public charging

- The Northwind Charge card and app give you access to more than 40,000 public charge points in the UK and 600,000 across Europe.
- Use the route planner in the car's navigation: it adds charging stops automatically and preconditions the battery for faster rapid charging.
- Rapid charging Ion 4 from 10% to 80% takes about 28 minutes at a 200 kW charger. Ion X takes about 30 minutes at a 250 kW charger.

## Battery care tips

- For daily driving, set the charge limit to 80%; charge to 100% before long trips.
- Avoid leaving the car at 100% or below 10% for several days.
- Rapid charging does not harm the battery, but home AC charging is gentler for everyday use.
- In winter, schedule departure times in the app so the car preheats the cabin and battery while still plugged in.
