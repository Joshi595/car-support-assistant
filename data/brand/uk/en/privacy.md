# Your Data and Privacy (United Kingdom)

Northwind Motors UK Ltd is the data controller for personal data processed in the UK, under the UK GDPR and the Data Protection Act 2018.

## What the car collects

- Vehicle status data: mileage, service needs, fault codes, battery and fuel level.
- Location data: only when you use navigation, Find My Car, the emergency call or roadside assistance, or when you allow it in the privacy settings.
- Driving data is never sold to insurers or third parties.

## Your privacy settings

In the car (Settings > Privacy) you can choose between three modes:
- Full: all connected services work.
- Limited: location is shared only for emergency call and roadside assistance.
- Offline: no data is sent, except for the legally required emergency call (eCall). Connected services and OTA updates do not work in this mode.

## Your rights

You can ask to access, correct, delete or export your personal data. Send requests to privacy@northwind-motors.example or use the privacy section of the Northwind Connect app. We respond within one month.

## Data Protection Officer

Contact our Data Protection Officer at dpo@northwind-motors.example. If you are unhappy with how we handle your data, you can complain to the Information Commissioner's Office (ICO).

## Selling your car

Before you sell your car, use Settings > Reset to factory settings to remove your profiles, phone pairings, saved addresses and digital keys, and remove the car from your Northwind Connect account.
