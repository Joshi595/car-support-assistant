# Complaints Process (United Kingdom)

We want to put things right quickly. If you are not happy with your car, a retailer or our service, please follow these steps.

## Step 1: Talk to your retailer

Most issues are best solved by the retailer that sold or serviced your car. Ask for the Customer Experience Manager. Retailers aim to respond within 5 working days.

## Step 2: Contact Northwind Customer Relations

If the retailer has not resolved your complaint, contact Northwind Customer Relations:
- Phone: 01632 960 000 (Monday to Friday 8:00 to 20:00, Saturday 9:00 to 17:00)
- Email: complaints@northwind-motors.example
- Post: Northwind Motors UK Customer Relations, PO Box 1000, Milton Keynes, MK1 1AA

We acknowledge every complaint within 2 working days and assign a named case manager. We aim to send a full response within 15 working days. If it will take longer, your case manager will tell you why and when to expect an answer.

## Step 3: Independent dispute resolution

If you are still not satisfied, or 8 weeks have passed without a final response, you can take your complaint to The Motor Ombudsman, the independent body for the UK motor industry. Northwind Motors UK is accredited to its codes of practice, and its service is free for consumers.

Complaints about finance agreements with Northwind Financial Services can be referred to the Financial Ombudsman Service after you have received our final response, or 8 weeks after you complained.

## What to include

- Your name, contact details and vehicle registration or VIN.
- A short description of the problem and what outcome you are looking for.
- Copies of any invoices, photos or previous correspondence.
