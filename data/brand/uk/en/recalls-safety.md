# Recalls and Safety Campaigns (United Kingdom)

## How you are informed

If your Northwind vehicle is affected by a safety recall, we contact you by letter at the address registered with the DVLA, and by a notification in the Northwind Connect app. Recalls are also published on the GOV.UK vehicle recall checker.

## Check if your car is affected

- Enter your registration number or VIN at northwind-motors.example/uk/recalls.
- Or call customer service on 01632 960 000.
- Your retailer also checks for open recalls at every service.

## What it costs

Recall repairs are always free of charge, regardless of the car's age, mileage or ownership history, and whether or not it is still under warranty.

## Over-the-air recall fixes

Some recalls are fixed by a software update delivered over the air. You will see a notification in the car and the app. Please install it as soon as possible.

## If you bought the car used

Recalls apply to the vehicle, not the owner. Make sure your address on the V5C is up to date with the DVLA so that recall letters reach you.

## Reporting a safety problem

If you think your Northwind has a safety defect, contact your retailer or customer service first. You can also report serious safety defects to the Driver and Vehicle Standards Agency (DVSA).
