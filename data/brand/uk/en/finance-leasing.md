# Finance and Leasing (United Kingdom)

Finance is provided by Northwind Financial Services UK Ltd, which is authorised and regulated by the Financial Conduct Authority. All finance is subject to status; you must be 18 or over and a UK resident.

## Personal Contract Purchase (PCP)

- Pay a deposit, then fixed monthly payments over 24 to 48 months.
- At the end, choose to pay the optional final payment and own the car, hand it back (subject to mileage and condition), or part-exchange it.
- Representative example (Ion 4 Long Range RWD): cash price £46,995, deposit £4,995, 47 monthly payments of £449, optional final payment £19,800, 8,000 miles per year, representative 6.9% APR.

## Hire Purchase (HP)

- Pay a deposit and fixed monthly payments over 12 to 60 months; you own the car after the last payment.
- No mileage limits.

## Personal Contract Hire (PCH, leasing)

- Fixed monthly rental over 24 to 48 months; you never own the car.
- Annual mileage from 5,000 to 20,000 miles. Excess mileage is charged at 12p per mile for Aero and Vela and 15p per mile for Ion models.
- Example: Aero Style, 36 months, 8,000 miles per year, initial rental £1,794 then 35 monthly rentals of £299.

## Business customers

Business Contract Hire and Northwind Fleet programmes are available. Contact fleet@northwind-motors.example.

## Settling early

You can settle a PCP or HP agreement early at any time. Request a settlement figure in the Northwind Financial Services portal or by calling 01632 960 300. Under a regulated agreement you also have the right to voluntary termination once you have paid half of the total amount payable.

## Missed payments

If you are having difficulty paying, contact us as early as possible on 01632 960 300. We can discuss payment arrangements, and we will never charge for that conversation.
