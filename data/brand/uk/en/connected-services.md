# Northwind Connect: App and Connected Services (United Kingdom)

## The Northwind Connect app

The free Northwind Connect app (iOS and Android) lets you:
- See fuel level or battery charge, range, mileage and service status.
- Lock and unlock the car, flash the lights and find the car on a map.
- Start and stop charging, set charge limits and departure times.
- Precondition the cabin (heat or cool) before you drive.
- Book a service and see your digital service history.

## Digital Key

- Use your iPhone, Apple Watch or a compatible Android phone as your car key.
- Share digital keys with up to 5 people. You can limit shared keys to certain times or set a top speed and a radio volume limit for new drivers.
- The physical key card is still supplied and works if your phone's battery is flat.
- Lost your phone? Disable the digital key immediately in the app on another device or by calling 01632 960 000.

## Software updates over the air

Northwind vehicles receive software updates over the air (OTA). Updates are downloaded in the background and installed when you confirm, which takes about 20 minutes while the car is parked. You cannot drive during installation. Safety-related recall fixes may also be delivered as OTA updates.

## Connected Plus subscription

Connected Plus includes live traffic, online search, streaming, a Wi-Fi hotspot (with a data plan) and remote preconditioning.
- Free for the first 3 years with every new car.
- After that: £9.99 per month or £99 per year.

## Emergency Call (eCall)

Every Northwind has an automatic emergency call system. In a serious accident, it calls the emergency services and sends the car's location even if nobody can speak. It works independently of any subscription and cannot be switched off.
