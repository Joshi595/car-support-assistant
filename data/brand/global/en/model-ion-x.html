Northwind Ion X: Model Guide

The Ion X is Northwind's all-electric large SUV with optional seven seats. It is sold in Germany, the United Kingdom and the United States.

Key specifications

  • Dual-motor all-wheel drive, 400 kW (544 hp), 860 Nm.
  • 0 to 100 km/h: 4.6 s (0 to 60 mph: 4.3 s). Top speed: 210 km/h (130 mph).
  • Battery: 100 kWh usable.
  • Range: 580 km WLTP (360 miles); 315 miles EPA in the United States.
  • Energy consumption: 19.2 kWh/100 km WLTP.

Charging

  • DC fast charging: up to 250 kW peak, 10 to 80% in about 30 minutes.
  • AC onboard charger: 11 kW (11.5 kW in the U.S.); 22 kW optional in Germany.
  • Charging port: CCS2 and Type 2 in Europe and the UK; NACS (SAE J3400) in the United States.

Vehicle-to-load (V2L)

The Ion X can power external electrical devices at up to 3.6 kW through the V2L adapter, which plugs into the charging port, and through a 230 V socket (120 V in the U.S.) in the boot. V2L stops automatically when the battery reaches the minimum level you set (default 20%).

Towing

  • Maximum braked trailer weight: 2,000 kg (4,400 lb in the U.S.).
  • Maximum nose weight: 100 kg.
  • Trailer mode adjusts the range estimate and suspension. Expect up to 40 to 50% less range when towing a heavy trailer.

Seating and space

  • 5 seats standard; a third row with 2 additional seats is optional (7 Seats Package).
  • Boot: 650 litres with 5 seats, 2,100 litres with second row folded.
  • Air suspension with adjustable ride height is standard.

Trims

  • Style: 20-inch wheels, panoramic roof, heated and ventilated front seats, 360-degree camera, Highway Assist.
  • Sport: adds 22-inch wheels, sports seats, rear-axle steering and an upgraded sound system.
