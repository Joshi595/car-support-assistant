# Northwind Aero: Model Guide

The Aero is Northwind's compact five-door hatchback for city and everyday driving. It is sold in Germany and the United Kingdom. The Aero is not available in the United States.

## Powertrain

- 1.5-litre three-cylinder turbocharged petrol engine with 48V mild-hybrid system.
- Power: 100 kW (136 hp). Torque: 230 Nm.
- 7-speed dual-clutch automatic transmission, front-wheel drive.
- 0 to 100 km/h: 8.9 seconds. Top speed: 205 km/h (127 mph).
- Fuel consumption (WLTP combined): 5.6 l/100 km (50.4 mpg). CO2: 127 g/km.
- Fuel: unleaded petrol, minimum 95 RON. E10 is approved. Tank capacity: 45 litres.

## Dimensions and practicality

- Length 4,260 mm, width 1,800 mm, height 1,450 mm.
- Boot capacity: 380 litres, 1,250 litres with rear seats folded.
- Maximum braked towing weight: 1,200 kg. Roof load: 75 kg.

## Trims

- Core: 17-inch alloy wheels, LED headlights, 10.25-inch touchscreen, Apple CarPlay and Android Auto (wireless), adaptive cruise control, rear parking sensors.
- Style: adds 18-inch wheels, heated front seats, rear-view camera, ambient lighting, keyless entry and digital key.
- Sport: adds sports suspension, 19-inch wheels, sports seats, black exterior trim and paddle shifters.

## Packages

- Comfort Package: heated steering wheel, electric driver's seat with memory, dual-zone climate control.
- Tech Package: head-up display, 360-degree camera, Harman Kardon sound system, wireless phone charging.
- Winter Package: heated windscreen, heated rear seats, heated steering wheel. In Germany the Winter Package can include a set of winter wheels.

## Driver assistance

All Aero models have autonomous emergency braking with pedestrian and cyclist detection, lane keeping assist, traffic sign recognition and a driver attention monitor. Style and Sport add blind spot monitoring and rear cross-traffic alert.
