# Northwind Vela: Model Guide

The Vela is a mid-size five-seat SUV with a plug-in hybrid powertrain. It is sold in Germany, the United Kingdom and the United States.

## Powertrain

- 2.0-litre four-cylinder turbocharged petrol engine plus an electric motor.
- System power: 220 kW (299 hp). System torque: 520 Nm.
- All-wheel drive, 8-speed automatic transmission.
- 0 to 100 km/h: 6.1 seconds (0 to 60 mph: 5.8 seconds).
- High-voltage battery: 22 kWh usable.
- Electric range: 85 km WLTP (53 miles WLTP); 38 miles EPA in the United States.
- Electric top speed: 140 km/h (87 mph).

## Driving modes

- Hybrid (default): the car chooses the best combination of engine and electric motor.
- Electric: drives on electricity only while there is enough charge.
- Save: holds the current battery charge for later, for example for a city at the end of a motorway trip.
- Charge: uses the engine to recharge the battery while driving (increases fuel consumption).

## Charging

- AC onboard charger: 7.4 kW (7.2 kW in the United States). 0 to 100% in about 3.5 hours.
- DC charging: up to 50 kW in Europe; 10 to 80% in about 25 minutes. DC charging is not available on U.S. models.

## Dimensions and practicality

- Length 4,750 mm, width 1,920 mm, height 1,680 mm.
- Boot capacity: 520 litres (slightly smaller than non-hybrid SUVs because the battery sits under the boot floor).
- Maximum braked towing weight: 2,000 kg (3,500 lb in the United States).
- Fuel tank: 55 litres (14.5 US gallons).

## Trims

- Core: 19-inch wheels, LED headlights, 12.3-inch touchscreen, heated front seats, power tailgate.
- Style: adds leather upholstery, 360-degree camera, panoramic roof, keyless entry and digital key.
- Sport: adds 21-inch wheels, adaptive air suspension and sports seats.

## Packages

- Tow Package: electrically retractable tow bar and trailer stability assist.
- Tech Package: head-up display, Harman Kardon sound, Highway Assist (hands-on lane centring at speeds up to 210 km/h / 130 mph).
- Winter Package: heated steering wheel, heated rear seats, heated windscreen.
