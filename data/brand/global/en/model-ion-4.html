Northwind Ion 4: Model Guide

The Ion 4 is Northwind's all-electric four-door sedan. It is sold in Germany, the United Kingdom and the United States.

Charging

  • DC fast charging: up to 200 kW peak. 10 to 80% in about 28 minutes.
  • AC onboard charger: 11 kW standard. In Germany, a 22 kW onboard charger is available as an option. U.S. models charge at up to 11.5 kW.
  • Charging port: CCS2 and Type 2 in Europe and the UK; NACS (SAE J3400) in the United States.
  • Plug & Charge: at compatible public chargers, charging starts automatically when you plug in, without a card or app.

Dimensions and practicality

  • Length 4,820 mm, width 1,880 mm, height 1,460 mm, wheelbase 2,900 mm.
  • Boot: 470 litres, plus a 50-litre front storage compartment (frunk).
  • Towing: up to 1,000 kg braked in Europe and the UK (tow bar option). Towing is not approved for U.S. models.

Trims

  • Core: 18-inch aero wheels, heat pump, 14.9-inch touchscreen, adaptive cruise control, heated front seats.
  • Style: adds 19-inch wheels, panoramic glass roof, ventilated front seats, 360-degree camera.
  • Sport (Performance AWD only): adds 20-inch wheels, adaptive suspension, sports seats and larger brakes.

Heat pump and winter range

Every Ion 4 has a heat pump, which uses waste heat from the drive system and outside air to heat the cabin and battery efficiently. In cold weather (around 0 °C / 32 °F), expect around 20 to 30% less range than the WLTP or EPA figure; preconditioning while plugged in reduces this loss.
